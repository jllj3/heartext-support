About HearText Captions

HearText Captions provides live speech-to-text captions for everyday conversations. The app is designed to make spoken communication easier to follow by displaying speech as clear, easy-to-read text.

Features

  • Live speech-to-text captions
  • Large, easy-to-read text
  • Microphone selection and audio monitoring
  • Saved conversations
  • Conversation sharing
