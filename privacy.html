Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 7, 2026

HearText Captions is designed to provide live speech-to-text captions for conversations. This privacy policy explains what information the app uses, what is stored on the device, and how that information is handled.

Speech and Audio

HearText Captions uses the device microphone and Apple's Speech Recognition service to provide live speech-to-text captions. Depending on the device and system capabilities, audio may be sent to Apple for speech recognition processing. HearText Captions does not operate its own speech-processing server and does not receive or store users' audio recordings on a HearText Captions server.

Saved Conversations

If you choose to save a conversation, the saved text is stored locally on your device. Saved conversations are not uploaded to a HearText Captions server.

Sharing

If you choose to share or export a saved conversation, the information is shared only through the destination or service you select using the device's standard sharing features.

Personal Information

HearText Captions does not require you to create an account and does not intentionally collect, sell, or share personal information for advertising purposes.

Analytics and Tracking

HearText Captions does not use third-party advertising trackers.

Children's Privacy

HearText Captions is not designed to collect personal information from children.

Changes to This Policy

This privacy policy may be updated from time to time as the app changes. The latest version will always be available on this page.
