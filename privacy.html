Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 7, 2026

HearText Captions is designed to provide live speech-to-text captions for conversations. This privacy policy explains how information is handled by the app.

Speech and Audio

HearText Captions uses the device microphone to provide live speech-to-text transcription. Audio is used only for the purpose of generating captions during use of the app.

Saved Conversations

If you choose to save a conversation, the saved text is stored locally on your device. Saved conversations are not uploaded to a HearText Captions server.

Sharing

If you choose to share or export a saved conversation, the information is shared only through the destination or service that you select using the device's standard sharing features.

Personal Information

HearText Captions does not require you to create an account and does not intentionally collect, sell, or share personal information for advertising purposes.

Analytics and Tracking

HearText Captions does not use third-party advertising trackers.

Children's Privacy

HearText Captions is not designed to collect personal information from children.

Changes to This Policy

This privacy policy may be updated from time to time as the app changes. The latest version will always be available on this page.
